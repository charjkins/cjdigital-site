    About

    I studied film production and now work in IT infrastructure. CJ Digital sits between the two: videos built in code, timed and finished like an edit.

    Each one starts from what's already there: the live website, the brand colours, the menu, the photography. Every number, name and line on screen comes from the source, no two videos share a template, and each gets its own sound design, mastered to play properly on a phone.

    Launching a site, a shop or a new feature? I'll turn it into a video like these.
